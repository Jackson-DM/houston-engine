# Command: Generate Memo

**Purpose:** Transform one high-priority signal or signal cluster into an executive insight memo.

---

# Workflow

1. Review the top signal candidate in `signals/triaged`
2. Identify the core business or industry shift represented by the signal
3. Synthesize the signal into an executive-level point of view
4. Draft a memo using the official memo schema
5. Save the draft to `memos/drafts`
6. Mark whether the memo is ready for content packaging or needs refinement

---

# Memo Requirements

Every memo should include:
- a strong executive title
- a concise executive summary
- the signal or signal cluster behind the memo
- a clear observation
- a strategic implication
- a forward-looking prediction
- a core strategic insight
- recommended content angles
- linked companies or accounts if relevant

---

# Writing Standard

The memo should not merely restate the news. It must explain:
- what is changing
- why it matters
- why it matters now
- why executives should care
- what this may lead to next

The memo should feel like insight, not reporting.

---

# Output

Save completed drafts to: `memos/drafts/`

**File naming pattern:** `YYYY-MM-DD_short-memo-slug.md`
**Example:** `2026-03-10_houston-industrial-ai-infrastructure.md`
