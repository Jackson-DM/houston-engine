# SIGNAL SCORING MODEL
Houston AI Authority Engine

---

# Purpose

The signal scoring model determines which incoming industry developments deserve deeper analysis and executive memo generation. Its purpose is to prevent the authority engine from reacting to every piece of news and instead prioritize the signals with the greatest strategic value.

The model helps the system identify developments that are:
- relevant to Houston
- important to executives
- capable of generating authority content
- likely to connect to consulting or business opportunity

---

# Scoring Philosophy

Not every signal should become content. The best signals are those that sit at the intersection of:
1. regional relevance
2. industry importance
3. executive urgency
4. authority-building potential
5. business opportunity

Signals should be evaluated based on quality, not volume.

---

# Scoring Criteria

Each signal is scored from 1 to 10 across five dimensions.

## 1. Houston Relevance
How strongly does this signal connect to Houston’s economy, industries, institutions, or innovation ecosystem?

Score high if the signal involves:
- Houston-based companies
- major local industries
- regional business implications
- ecosystem momentum in Houston

---

## 2. Industry Importance
How significant is the development within its industry?

Score high if the signal reflects:
- major company movement
- meaningful adoption trends
- new market direction
- operational transformation

---

## 3. Executive Urgency
How likely is this signal to matter right now to operators, founders, or decision-makers?

Score high if the signal suggests:
- competitive pressure
- risk of falling behind
- immediate strategic decision-making
- clear operational consequences

---

## 4. Authority Potential
How strong is this signal as the basis for insight-led authority content?

Score high if the signal enables:
- contrarian or sharp insight
- thought leadership positioning
- useful explanation of what changes next
- strong executive framing

---

## 5. Consulting Opportunity
How likely is this signal to connect to future advisory, implementation, or strategic services?

Score high if the signal suggests demand for:
- AI strategy
- workflow implementation
- automation consulting
- operational modernization
- executive education

---

# Total Score

Each signal receives a score out of 50.

Formula:
Total Signal Score = Houston Relevance + Industry Importance + Executive Urgency + Authority Potential + Consulting Opportunity

---

# Signal Priority Bands

## Tier 1: High Priority
Score: 40–50
These signals should be strongly considered for memo generation. They are likely to produce high-value insight, strong authority content, and possible business relevance.

## Tier 2: Medium Priority
Score: 30–39
These signals may be useful, but should be compared against stronger alternatives before becoming memos.

## Tier 3: Low Priority
Score: Below 30
These signals should usually be archived unless they support a stronger signal cluster.

---

# Signal Evaluation Questions

Before approving a signal for memo generation, ask:
- Why does this matter now?
- Why does this matter in Houston?
- Why should an executive care?
- What insight can be extracted from this beyond simple reporting?
- Could this topic lead to consulting relevance or business opportunity?

If the signal cannot produce a clear answer to these questions, it should not become a memo.

# Example Scoring

## Example Signal
Chevron expands AI-assisted predictive maintenance in refinery operations

Houston Relevance: 10
Industry Importance: 9
Executive Urgency: 8
Authority Potential: 9
Consulting Opportunity: 9

Total Score: 45
Priority Band: Tier 1

Reason: This signal is regionally relevant, strategically important, operationally meaningful, and directly connected to AI adoption in Houston’s industrial base.

---

# Operating Rule

The authority engine should prioritize the strongest signals, not the most recent signals. Recency matters, but strategic value matters more.

---

# Role in the Phase 4 System

The signal scoring model sits between:
raw signal collection and executive memo generation

Pipeline:
signals/raw → scoring and triage → signals/triaged → memo generation → content packaging

This model is the decision layer that turns signal collection into signal intelligence.
