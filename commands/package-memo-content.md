# Command: Package Memo into Content

**Purpose:** Convert an approved executive memo into authority content assets. This step transforms research insight into public-facing authority content.

---

# Workflow

1. Review the memo in `memos/approved`
2. Identify the strongest insight from the memo
3. Generate multiple authority content angles
4. Produce LinkedIn-ready posts
5. Store the output inside the content directory
6. Log relevant companies or engagement opportunities in CRM if applicable

---

# Content Requirements

Each memo should generate:
• 3–5 LinkedIn posts
• optional Twitter/X thread
• optional newsletter concept
• optional executive talking point

---

# Writing Standard

Authority content should focus on:
• explaining industry change
• highlighting implications
• providing operator-level insight

Avoid:
• generic summaries
• obvious commentary
• promotional tone

The goal is to demonstrate understanding of **where the industry is moving**.

---

# Output

Store generated posts in: `content/linkedin-posts/`

**File naming pattern:** `YYYY-MM-DD_memo-topic_posts.md`
**Example:** `2026-03-15_energy-ai-infrastructure_posts.md`
