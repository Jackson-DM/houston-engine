# Weekly Operating Rhythm
Houston AI Authority Engine

---

# Weekly Intelligence Cycle

The authority engine operates on a weekly signal intelligence cycle.

---

# Monday — Signal Collection

Collect potential signals from:
• industry news
• social media
• operator conversations
• ecosystem developments
• company announcements

Store signals in: `signals/raw`

---

# Tuesday — Signal Triage

Evaluate signals using the official scoring model.

Move strongest signals into: `signals/triaged`

---

# Wednesday — Memo Generation

Convert top signals into executive insight memos.

Store drafts in: `memos/drafts`
Approved memos move to: `memos/approved`

---

# Thursday — Content Packaging

Convert approved memos into authority content.

Store output in: `content/linkedin-posts`

---

# Friday — CRM and Analytics

Log relevant companies and opportunities.

Track content performance in: `analytics/content-performance.md`

---

# Objective

Repeat this cycle weekly to maintain a consistent authority pipeline driven by real industry signals.
