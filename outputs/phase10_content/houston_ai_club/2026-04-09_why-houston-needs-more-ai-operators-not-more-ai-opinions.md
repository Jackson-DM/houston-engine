What we are starved for are AI operators.

These are the engineers, the process architects, and the domain experts who look at a clunky, manual workflow in a refinery or a clinic and say, I can automate that with an agentic system.

Take the recent success stories coming out of our industrial sectors, where companies are leveraging tools like ChatGPT Enterprise not for novelty, but for massive, measurable productivity gains in R&D and industrial safety. That is not theory. That is operational excellence. When you see an 80 percent productivity lift in a high-stakes environment, you are not looking at a chatbot gimmick. To win here, you need to understand how to bridge the gap between a raw model and a production-grade workflow.
