# MEMO SCHEMA
Houston AI Authority Engine

---

# Purpose

The memo schema defines the structure for executive insight generation inside the authority engine. Memos are the core intellectual artifact of Phase 4. They translate high-priority signals into a clear strategic point of view that can be used for:
- authority content
- internal research
- executive education
- consulting relevance
- CRM opportunity tracking

---

# Memo Philosophy

A strong memo does not simply summarize events. A strong memo identifies the deeper pattern behind the signal and explains why that pattern matters.

The memo should help an executive understand:
- what is happening
- what it means
- what changes next
- what deserves attention now

---

# Required Memo Sections

## 1. Title
A concise, executive-facing title that frames the core insight.
Example: AI Is Becoming Core Infrastructure in Houston Energy Operations

---

## 2. Date
The date the memo was generated.

---

## 3. Signal Cluster
The underlying signal or related set of signals that triggered the memo. This may include:
- one major signal
- several supporting signals
- a pattern across multiple developments

---

## 4. Executive Summary
A short summary of the memo’s core point. This should be readable in under 30 seconds.

---

## 5. Observation
What is changing? Describe the most important development or pattern visible in the signal cluster.

---

## 6. Implication
Why does this matter? Explain the business, operational, strategic, or ecosystem consequence.

---

## 7. Prediction
What is likely to happen next? This should be a grounded forward-looking statement, not hype.

---

## 8. Strategic Insight
What is the strongest point of view the authority engine can extract from this signal? This is the most important section in the memo.

---

## 9. Content Angles
List 3–5 possible authority content directions that could be created from the memo.
Examples:
- executive LinkedIn post
- contrarian insight post
- Houston ecosystem angle
- operator lesson
- consulting relevance angle

---

## 10. Linked Companies
List the companies, sectors, or target accounts most connected to the memo theme.

---

# Writing Standard

The memo should be:
- concise
- strategic
- readable by executives
- grounded in signal evidence
- useful for downstream content creation

Avoid:
- generic summaries
- hype language
- vague observations
- repetitive phrasing
- obvious takeaways with no strategic edge

---

# Role in the Pipeline

signals/raw → signals/triaged → memo generation → memos/drafts → memos/approved → content packaging

The memo is the bridge between information and authority.
