## LinkedIn Post 2 — From Predictive to Agentic Energy

For a decade, "AI in Energy" meant one thing: Predictive Maintenance.

Sensors tell you when a pump might fail. Useful? Yes. Revolutionary? No.

The real shift happening in Houston right now is moving from *predictive* to *agentic*.

We’re seeing operators move toward systems that don't just "flag" an issue, but:
1. Research the specific equipment manual.
2. Draft the work order.
3. Check the inventory for the replacement part.
4. Prepare the safety briefing for the crew.

This is the shift from "AI as a dashboard" to "AI as a teammate."

If you’re still treating AI as a data science project, you’re missing the operational leverage.

## LinkedIn Post 4 — Logistics: Solving the "Information Fog"

The Port of Houston is a masterclass in complexity.

Trucks, ships, rail, and warehouses—all trying to sync in real-time.

This is where Houston-based logistics firms are quietly winning with AI.

They aren't replacing trucks. They are replacing the friction of moving them.

By using AI agents to monitor "foggy" data points—port congestion, weather delays, customs paperwork—they are reclaiming hours of lost time every single day.

1. Narrow the focus: Pick ONE workflow (e.g., Invoice Reconciliation or Safety Reporting).

3. Build the Library: Turn every successful prompt and workflow into a company asset.

Houston has the scale. We have the data. We have the domain expertise.

The only thing left is the "Double Down"—moving from "testing" AI to "integrating" AI into the core operating model.
